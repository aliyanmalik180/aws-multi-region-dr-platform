Projects

Multi-region disaster recovery on AWS

A three-tier app that survives the loss of an entire AWS region. Aurora replicates to us-west-2, a pilot-light copy of the app stays ready, and Route53 health checks move traffic automatically. Terraform, EKS, ArgoCD and GitHub Actions (OIDC) deliver it to both regions.
